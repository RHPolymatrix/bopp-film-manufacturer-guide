# BOPP Film Manufacturer Guide

## Overview

This repository provides the **BOPP Film Manufacturer: Packaging Applications & Buyer Guide**, published by **RH Polymatrix**.

The guide is created for packaging manufacturers, purchase departments, converters, printers, plastic processors and B2B buyers who source BOPP films for industrial and packaging applications.

It explains the key properties and uses of BOPP film, factors to consider when selecting suitable film, common BOPP film types, supplier evaluation and practical sourcing considerations.

The guide also helps buyers understand the difference between BOPP and BOPA packaging films and evaluate material options according to their application requirements.

## BOPP Film Applications

BOPP films are used across various packaging and industrial segments, including:

- Food and snack packaging
- Flexible packaging
- Printing and lamination
- Labels and stickers
- FMCG packaging
- Pharmaceutical packaging
- Stationery applications
- Textile packaging
- Industrial wrapping
- Gift wrapping

The suitable film type depends on the intended use, packaging process and required performance.
